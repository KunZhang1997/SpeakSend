# SpeakSend

**Voice input and recording workbench for macOS.**  
Hold `Fn` to dictate clean text straight into any app, turn long meetings and spoken drafts into verifiable notes, and let months-old ideas automatically connect into a network.

### 1. Global Voice Input — Speak at Your Cursor, Trim Filler Words

Hold `Fn` inside AI chatboxes, Slack, WeChat, email, or any editor and release to insert clean text right where your cursor is — stripping "uh" and "um" while keeping your natural tone and custom vocabulary intact.

![Global Voice Input](docs/images/v0.1.1/02-voice-input.png)

### 2. Long Recordings & Hybrid Notes — Click Any Takeaway to Check the Quote

Type a quick outline and click the mic whenever you want to talk it out, or record both sides of online meetings even with headphones on. Click any decision or action item on the left to jump straight to the exact spoken quote on the right.

![Long Recording & Hybrid Workbench](docs/images/v0.1.1/03-meeting-workbench.png)

### 3. Idea Network — Old Thoughts Connect Automatically Across Folders

No manual tags or backlinks required. Casual walk notes and meeting reviews automatically link related topics across folders and months, with every original quote one click away.

![Idea Network](docs/images/v0.1.1/04-idea-network.png)

### 4. Offline Models & Plain Markdown — Runs on Your Mac, Zero Per-Minute Fees

Download free on-device `Qwen3-ASR` or `Whisper` speech models and pair with local `Ollama` structuring so private audio never leaves your Mac. Every note is saved as plain `.md` files ready for Finder or Obsidian.

![Offline Models & Local Markdown](docs/images/v0.1.1/05-local-native.png)
